# Store listing

Listing text for the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons. The name and summary come from the manifest (`public/_locales/en/messages.json`); keep them in step.

## Name

Chrono – Finish Time & Speed for YouTube

## Summary (both stores)

See how long a YouTube video really takes at your speed, and when you'll finish.

## Description (both stores)

How long will this video actually take at 1.5×? When will I finish this playlist? What speed do I need to be done by 6 PM?

Chrono answers these right inside YouTube.

PANEL
A compact block at the top of YouTube's sidebar, styled like YouTube's own panels. At a glance: the video's remaining watch time at your speed, its finish time, and speed − / + buttons. Expand it for more:
• Speed table: remaining and finish time at every common speed.
• Chapter: remaining and finish time for the chapter you're in.
• Playlist: remaining and finish time up to any entry you pick.
• Targets: set a finish time or the watch time you have, and Chrono shows the required speed.

PLAYER BADGE
An optional "· ends 3:45 PM" after the player's own time, visible in fullscreen too.

PLAYLIST SUMMARY
One line on playlist pages: total content time, watch time at your speed, and how many entries are unavailable.

ALSO
• Chrono's speed is always YouTube's speed. Change it in either place.
• Works with videos opened in background tabs, and as you move between videos.
• Follows YouTube's light or dark theme, and its theater and fullscreen modes.
• Pauses during ad breaks.
• 12- or 24-hour clock, or automatic.

PRIVACY
Chrono collects no data. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. It needs only storage for your settings and access to www.youtube.com.

Chrono is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## Privacy declarations

- Chrome Web Store: "This extension does not collect or use your data."
  - Single purpose: show watch time and finish times for YouTube videos at the viewer's speed.
  - `storage`: keeps the viewer's settings.
  - Host access to www.youtube.com: where Chrono shows its Panel, Player badge and Playlist summary.
- Firefox Add-ons: `data_collection_permissions: none` is declared in the manifest.
